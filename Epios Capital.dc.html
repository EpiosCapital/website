04  /  ABOUT

Why we trade in the open.

Epios Capital is a small, systematic firm. We build models, trade liquid markets, and let the track record speak. Nothing here is dressed up.

The industry runs on opacity — vague mandates, hidden books, performance shown only when it flatters. We think that erodes trust in markets and in the people who run them. So we do the opposite: we publish our AUM, the instruments we trade, and where the money goes, every single month.

Transparency isn't a marketing line for us. It's a constraint we hold ourselves to — the part of the business that can be seen, should be seen.

05  /  GIVING
FUTURE PLANS

Profit, pointed at the root of suffering.

As Epios Capital grows, all profits above operation costs and necessary reinvesting into the fund will go to charitable causes in mental health and well-being. This will be tracked here once Epios Capital reaches the stage where it is ready for this.

The premise of the giving is simple and uncomfortable: much of the suffering in the world traces back to untreated mental illness — to children who grow up in unstable, frightening homes, and to the ego-driven, wounded adults those childhoods produce.

Our first focus is upstream of all of it: helping parents build and hold a stable environment for their children. A childhood that is safe, calm, and loved is the single best intervention we know of — it sets a person on a brighter path before the damage is ever done.

The foundation is not yet established. This page describes where we're headed; specifics will be published here as it's formed.
